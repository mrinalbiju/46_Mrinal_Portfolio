About me

I’m a final-year Computer Science student at Mar Baselios College of Engineering and Technology, drawn to problems where machine learning meets a real interface — something a person can actually open and use.

Most of what I’ve built has been end-to-end: the crowd-panic detection system took me from OpenCV and CSRNet through to a Flask dashboard security staff could read at a glance; SafeShelf took me from an async PostgreSQL schema to a barcode scanner running on a phone. I like owning that whole path, and I use AI-assisted tooling heavily to move quickly through the parts that aren’t the interesting problem.

What I’m looking for

A team building practical, intelligent products where I can work across the stack and learn from people who ship.
